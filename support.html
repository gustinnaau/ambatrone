Start with the essentials

A few quick checks solve many first-flight and connection problems.

Before your first flight

Charge the aircraft and controller, install the compatible flight app, and complete setup outdoors in an open area.

  • Remove the gimbal cover before powering on.
  • Check propellers for cracks or looseness.
  • Wait for a strong positioning signal before takeoff.

Connection or pairing trouble?

Restart the aircraft and controller, confirm the app and firmware are current, then follow the pairing sequence for your model.

See connection answers →

Battery not charging?

Use the supplied charger, let a hot or cold battery return to room temperature, and inspect the charging contacts for dirt or damage.

Repairs & service

Gathering a few details first helps a service team understand the issue sooner.

1. Make the aircraft safe

Power it off, remove the battery if it is safe to do so, and do not charge a swollen, wet, or visibly damaged battery.

2. Collect the details

Note your model, serial number, purchase date, and what happened. Photos or a short video can help explain visible damage.

3. Keep your accessories

Back up footage and settings. Do not ship batteries or accessories unless the service team asks you to.

Frequently asked questions

Search above to narrow these answers. Open a question to read more.

Why will my drone not connect to the controller?

Charge both devices, ensure you are using the controller intended for your model, and restart the aircraft, controller, and app. If they still do not pair, follow the model-specific pairing instructions in its user guide.

What should I do if the battery will not charge?

Use the recommended charger and check that the contacts are clean and dry. Let a battery that is very hot or cold return to room temperature. Stop using it if it is swollen, leaking, unusually hot, or damaged.

How do I update firmware safely?

Check the product's official app or support materials for the latest compatible firmware. Charge the aircraft and controller, keep them powered and near each other during the update, and do not interrupt the process.

Where can I find my serial number?

Look for a product label on the aircraft or packaging, or check the device information screen in the flight app. The exact location varies by model.

What information should I include in a repair request?

Include the model and serial number, purchase date, a clear description of the issue, any app error messages, and photos of visible damage. Never send a damaged battery unless the service team gives safe shipping instructions.

Can I fly while an app or firmware update is available?

For the safest and most predictable operation, review the release notes and install required updates before flying. Do not begin an update when you need the aircraft immediately; keep it stationary until the update completes.
